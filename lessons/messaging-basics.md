# Lesson 5: Messaging Basics

Staying in touch with family and friends is easier than ever. You might see different apps on your phone for sending messages. It is normal to feel a little confused by the choices. You cannot break your phone by trying them out.

## Types of Messages

There are two main ways phones send written notes:

1. **Standard Text Message (SMS):** This stands for Short Message Service. It uses your regular phone plan to send words. 
2. **Internet Message:** Apps like WhatsApp or iMessage use your Wi-Fi or cell phone data instead of your phone plan. They let you send pictures and videos much faster. They are usually free, even if you message someone in another country.

## Group Chats

Sometimes you want to talk to the whole family at once. 

1. A **Group Chat** is a message where three or more people can talk together in one shared room. 
2. When you send a message in a group chat, everyone in the group sees it. 
3. When anyone replies, everyone sees the reply. 

## Voice Notes

Typing on a small screen can be tiring. You can send a recording of your voice instead.

1. A **Voice Note** is a short audio recording you send exactly like a text message.
2. Open a message to a friend.
3. Look for a small microphone icon next to the box where you normally type your message.
4. Press and hold your finger on the microphone icon.
5. Speak your message out loud while holding your finger down.
6. Let go of the icon to send it. 

*(If you make a mistake while holding it down, you can usually slide your finger to the side to cancel it. Do not worry if you send it by accident!)*

## Try it yourself

1. Open your favorite messaging app.
2. Find a conversation with a family member or close friend.
3. Look for the microphone icon.
4. Press and hold it, say "Hello, I am practicing sending a voice note!", and let go.

## Printable Summary: Messaging Basics

* **Standard Text Message (SMS):** A basic text sent using your regular phone plan.
* **Internet Message:** A text sent using Wi-Fi or data (like WhatsApp or iMessage). Great for sending photos and talking internationally for free.
* **Group Chat:** A shared message room where three or more people can talk at the same time.
* **Voice Note:** A spoken audio recording sent in a message.
* **How to send a voice note:** Press and hold the microphone icon next to the typing area, speak your message, and let go to send.
