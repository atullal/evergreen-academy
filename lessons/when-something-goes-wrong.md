# Lesson 10: When Something Goes Wrong

Even the most advanced computers and phones get confused sometimes. When a screen freezes or an app stops working, it is normal to worry that you pressed the wrong button. Please remember: you did not break it, and it is rarely permanent. Today, we will learn two simple tools to fix common problems.

## The Magic of Restarting

If a device is acting strangely, the best first step is to turn it off and turn it back on. 

1. A **Restart** means turning your phone or computer completely off, waiting a few seconds, and turning it back on.
2. Think of your device like a busy kitchen. If you are cooking a big meal, the counters get messy and it is hard to work. 
3. Restarting is like wiping the counters clean so you can start fresh. It gives the computer a chance to clear its memory and try again.
4. Using restart is a normal, healthy tool for your devices. It does not mean you made a mistake.
5. Turning off a device usually involves holding the power button on the side, or sometimes holding the power button and a volume button at the exact same time. 
6. Because every phone is slightly different, the best thing to do is ask a trusted family member or friend to show you the specific "restart" buttons for your phone the next time you see them.

## Making Room: Storage Full

Sometimes, your device might show a message that says "Storage Almost Full". 

1. **Storage** is the physical space inside your device where it keeps your pictures, messages, and apps. Think of it like a filing cabinet or a closet.
2. When the closet is completely packed, you cannot put anything new inside. Your phone will not be able to take new pictures or receive new messages.
3. To fix this, you just need to clear out some space.
4. The easiest way to make space is to delete things you no longer need, like old photographs and long videos. 
5. **Safety tip:** Deleting things can feel scary! If you need to do a major clean-up to make space, ask a family member or friend to sit with you and help. 
6. Do not worry if you make a mistake: most phones have a "Recently Deleted" or "Trash" folder where deleted photos wait for about a month before disappearing forever.

## Try it yourself

1. The next time you see a trusted friend or family member, ask them: "Can you show me the exact buttons to restart my phone?"
2. Write those steps down on a small piece of paper.
3. Open your photos app.
4. Find one blurry picture or a picture of something you no longer need (like a receipt from last year).
5. Delete that one picture to practice making space in your storage. Remember, it goes to a "Recently Deleted" folder, so you cannot permanently break anything!

## Printable Summary: When Something Goes Wrong

* **You did not break it:** Frozen screens and strange glitches are normal. They are not your fault.
* **Restart:** Turning a device completely off and back on again. It is a normal tool to give the device a fresh start, like cleaning a messy kitchen counter.
* **Storage:** The physical space inside your phone where things are kept, like a closet.
* **Storage Full:** This means the closet is full. You cannot save new things until you remove old things.
* **How to fix full storage:** The easiest way to make room is to find and delete old, blurry, or unwanted photos and videos. If you need to do a lot of cleaning, ask someone you trust for help. Mistakes are okay; deleted photos usually stay in a "Recently Deleted" folder for a month!
