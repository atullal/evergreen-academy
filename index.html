Evergreen Digital Academy
Free, patient technology lessons for older adults

Learn at your own pace.
Nothing here can break.

These lessons are for people who did not grow up with the internet — written slowly, in plain words, with every term explained. They are free, with nothing to buy, no account to create, and no information collected about you.

Start Learning Now

A promise before you start

You are not "bad with technology". Technology is often bad with people. Every lesson here assumes you are smart and capable — just new to this, and that is a fine place to be. Print any lesson and learn on paper if you prefer.

Available Lessons

  • 📱 Getting to know your smartphone

    The three touches, the home screen, and the one move that always rescues you. Start here if you are brand new.

  • 🛑 Spotting scams: the golden rules

    Urgency + secrecy + money = stop. One pattern that protects you from almost every scam, old or new.

  • 👋 Video calls with your family

    See the people you love, not just hear them. Receiving, calling, and surviving the mute button.

  • 📸 Sending photos to family

    Taking a picture and sharing it is a wonderful way to stay connected. Find the camera, take the photo, and share it safely.

  • 💬 Messaging basics

    Staying in touch with family and friends is easier than ever. Learn about types of messages, group chats, and how to send voice notes.

  • 🔑 Passwords without tears

    Keeping track of passwords can be frustrating. Learn how to make passwords easier to remember and much safer to use.

  • 💳 Paying safely online

    Learn how to make safe choices when buying things on the internet and how to spot common payment tricks.

  • 🏛️ Using government services online

    Learn how to safely access official government portals and avoid copycat websites designed to steal your information.

  • 📧 Email confidently

    Learn how to use email safely and confidently. Read and reply to messages, understand attachments, and spot phishing scams.

  • 🛠️ When something goes wrong

    Even the most advanced computers get confused. Learn two simple tools to fix common problems: restarting and freeing up storage.

Interactive Practice

  • 🛡️ Practice Inbox: Spot the Scam

    A safe, interactive environment to practice spotting scam emails. No real money or personal information is connected here—try it out without fear!

  • 🛒 Practice Checkout: Safe Payments

    A zero-risk sandbox where you can practice checking website addresses for tricks and choosing the safest payment method.

  • 📱 Practice Text Messages: Golden Rule

    A safe space to practice reading text messages, looking for signs of scams, and safely deleting them without fear.
